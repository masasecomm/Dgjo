Public Service Vacancy Circulars

Find DPSA vacancy circulars and government job adverts

The DPSA circular collects vacancy advertisements from public service departments. Use the official publication to find an advert, then follow the hiring department’s requirements and deadline.

Open the official DPSA circular page ↗

Official source: The DPSA Public Service Vacancy Circular page explains that the circular is generally published weekly, except during December. Check that page for current publications and official forms.

How to use a vacancy circular

  1. Open the relevant circular from the official DPSA page.
  2. Find the department and post that match your experience.
  3. Read the complete advert, including qualifications, reference number, documents and closing date.
  4. Apply using the method and contact details stated by the department advertising the vacancy.

Check details with the hiring department

The circular contains advertisements from different departments. The department named in an advert is responsible for that post, so direct vacancy questions and your application to that department rather than assuming DPSA handles recruitment for every listing.

Prepare the application form

Use the current Z83 application form and read the advert for any supporting documents or extra steps. Download the official editable Z83 form from DPSA, then follow the department’s submission instructions.

For a step-by-step checklist, see how to apply for DPSA vacancies.

This is an independent guide and is not affiliated with or endorsed by DPSA. Verify current vacancy details and deadlines in the official advert.
