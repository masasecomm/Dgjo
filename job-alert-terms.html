Job alerts and CV matching

Terms

Please read these details before signing up for job alerts or using CV matching.

Job alerts

Job alerts are optional. To subscribe, submit the separate job-alert form and confirm your email address using the message we send you. Alerts cover newly published vacancies across provinces and departments. Uploading a CV does not subscribe you to job alerts.

CV matching and your information

CV matching reads your selected CV file in your browser to extract text; the original CV file is not uploaded to or stored by this website. If you choose to match your CV and accept the terms, your name, email address and extracted CV text are sent to our CV matching service and retained to record and manage the request. Do not submit a CV if you do not agree to this use of your information.

Job-alert signup collects your name, email address, alert preferences, subscription status and related signup and status times to manage your subscription and send alerts. You can unsubscribe using the link in any alert email. Unsubscribing stops future alerts but does not automatically delete the associated record.

Vacancy information and liability

This is an independent guide and is not affiliated with or endorsed by DPSA or the South African Government. We make reasonable efforts to present vacancy information accurately, but listings may contain errors, omissions or outdated details, and requirements or closing dates may change. The official advert and hiring department’s instructions take precedence. Check them before relying on a listing or submitting an application.

To the extent permitted by law, this website is provided without a guarantee that vacancy information is complete, current or error-free. We are not liable for loss arising from reliance on inaccurate or outdated listing information, missed deadlines or application outcomes, except where liability cannot lawfully be excluded.
