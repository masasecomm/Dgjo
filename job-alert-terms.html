Job alerts and CV matching

Terms

Please read these details before signing up for job alerts or using CV matching.

How job alerts work

When you submit the signup form, we send a confirmation email to the address you provide. Alerts begin only after you confirm your email address. We send alerts about newly published vacancies across all provinces and departments.

Information we collect

With your agreement, we collect your name, email address, all-provinces and all-departments alert preferences, subscription status, and related signup and status timestamps. These details are recorded in the website owner’s DPSAWebsite Google Sheet and are used to manage your subscription and send job alerts.

CV matching

If you use CV matching and agree to these terms, your name, email address, and text extracted from your uploaded CV are sent to the website owner’s DPSAWebsite Google Sheet and used to compare your experience with published vacancies. The CV file is processed in your browser and is not uploaded; extracted CV text and contact details are stored in the sheet. The sheet record is not automatically deleted.

Unsubscribing and retention

You can unsubscribe using the link in an alert email. Unsubscribing stops further alert emails and updates your subscription status in the sheet. It does not automatically delete the record. The website owner may remove records manually.

About vacancy information

Job alerts are provided as a convenience by an independent guide. Vacancy details, eligibility and closing dates can change. Always check the full official department advert before applying.
