Helpful answers

DPSA jobs: frequently asked questions

How often are public service vacancy circulars published?

The DPSA says its Public Service Vacancy Circular is generally published weekly, except during December. Check the official DPSA page for current publications.

Where do I send an application for a DPSA vacancy?

Send your application to the department advertising the vacancy, using the contact or submission method in that advert. Direct vacancy questions to that department.

Is this the official DPSA jobs website?

No. This is an independent information guide and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the South African Government or DPSA. Verify every vacancy, closing date and application instruction against the official department advert.
