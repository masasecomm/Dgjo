Application checklist

How to apply for DPSA vacancies

Public service application requirements vary by department and role. Use this checklist to prepare, but treat the official vacancy advert as the final instruction.

1. Read the complete advert

Confirm the job title, department, reference number, location, minimum requirements and closing date. Note whether the department asks for a Z83 form, CV, certified documents or another submission step.

2. Complete the Z83 application form

Download the editable Z83 form from the official DPSA website. Complete the required fields accurately and check the advert for the version or additional guidance the department requires.

3. Prepare the requested supporting information

Include a CV and any other documents explicitly requested in the advert. Make sure your application identifies the correct post and reference number. Do not assume that one department’s requirements apply to another vacancy.

4. Submit before the closing date

Send your application to the department advertising the post using only the email address, online portal or postal details given in the advert. Allow time for delivery and retain a copy for your records.

Need help with a specific advert? The DPSA notes that enquiries about an advertised post should be directed to the department where the vacancy exists. Check the official DPSA vacancy circular page and contact the hiring department using its published details.

This site is an independent guide, not an official government service. Always verify the post, documents, application method and deadline against the original department advert.
