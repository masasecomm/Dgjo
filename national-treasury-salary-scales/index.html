National Treasury Salary Scales

A readable guide to the figures in the supplied Salary Adjustment Table PDF for full-time Public Service Act employees on salary levels 1–12.

Download the original PDF
How to read this table: The figures below are monthly amounts. The PDF describes the proposed monthly notch as including pension and service bonus. Overall monthly salary combines the proposed notch with the listed housing allowance, medical subsidy and pay progression where shown. The after-PAYE figure is an estimate based on annualising that total and applying 2027 SARS rates for an under-65 taxpayer with the primary rebate. It excludes UIF, GEPF/pension contributions, medical aid credits and other deductions, so it is not guaranteed take-home pay.

What the supplied table shows

5.8%Cost-of-living adjustment used for the guaranteed salary increase across levels 1–12.
R900 → R1 100Proposed monthly housing allowance for levels 1–10: a potential increase of R200 (22.2%).
R2 760 → R3 545Proposed monthly medical subsidy for levels 1–10: a potential increase of R785 (28.5%).
